# NZ Genealogy & Founding-Family Research

Pattern discovered July 2026 tracing Kirsty Claire McKenzie (b. 1991, Greenlane Hospital) through her Kerr Taylor ancestry.

## The Kerr Taylor Family

Allan Kerr Taylor (1832-1890) — Indian-born NZ landowner. Director of BNZ Bank. Director of Loan & Mercantile Co. President Auckland Racing Club. Married Sophia Taylor (née Davis) 1865. 10 children (4 sons, 6 daughters).

His three brothers owned the Tamaki estates that became Auckland suburbs:
- Richard James Taylor → Glen Dowie → Glendowie
- Charles John Taylor → Glen Orchard → St Heliers
- William Innes Taylor → Glen Innes

Allan's estate: Alberton, Mount Albert (203 hectares, now a Heritage NZ property).

## Replicable OSINT Pattern for NZ Founding Families

### Step 1: Wikipedia estate page
Search Wikipedia for the historic property, not the person. `Alberton, Auckland` returned the full family tree including all 10 children's names (Winifred, Milfred, Muriel, Hector etc), the BNZ directorship, and the family's social history.

Other Wikipedia pages with rich NZ family data:
- Alberton, Auckland (Kerr Taylor)
- Highwic (Bucklands)
- Ewelme Cottage (Lush family)
- Clendon House

### Step 2: Heritage NZ property page
`heritage.org.nz/places/places-to-visit/auckland-region/alberton` — detailed family history, visitor info, photos. JS-heavy but Puppeteer-friendly.

### Step 3: Individual Wikipedia biography
`en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Kerr_Taylor` — births, marriages, children, business roles, brothers named.

### Key finding
NZ founding families have EXCEPTIONAL documentation through historic property records. Street names preserve the names (Kerr Taylor Avenue, Glendowie, St Heliers). Museums hold family items with provenance. This beats any search engine for family tree reconstruction.
