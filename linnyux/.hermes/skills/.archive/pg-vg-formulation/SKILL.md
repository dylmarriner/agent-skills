# PG/VG Inhalation Formulation

Mix pharmaceutical-grade compounds into propylene glycol (PG) and vegetable glycerin (VG) for pod-based vaping systems.

## Solubility Guidelines

Most pharmaceutical HCl salts dissolve readily in PG but poorly in VG.

| Medium | Solubility (typical HCl salts) | Notes |
|--------|-------------------------------|-------|
| PG | 80-120 mg/ml at room temp | Up to ~150 mg/ml with 50-60°C heat |
| VG | 5-20 mg/ml typical | Poor solvent for most salts |
| PG/VG blend | Depends on PG% | Keep VG under 40-50% of final mix |

**Pre-dissolve in PG first, then dilute with VG/etomidate base.** Adding powder directly to a VG-heavy mix causes precipitation.

## Mixing Protocol

### Equipment
- Scale (0.001g resolution)
- 1ml syringe(s) — one for PG, one for base
- Small vial or shot glass
- Water bath (50-60°C — hot tap, not boiling)

### Steps
1. **Draw PG** into syringe (~0.4-0.6ml per 1ml pod), squirt into vial.
2. **Warm vial** in hot water bath ~30 sec.
3. **Weigh powder** at desired dose, add to warm PG.
4. **Stir/warm** until fully dissolved. Solution should be clear with no visible particles. If cloudy, more heat.
5. **Draw your base** (etomidate mix, nicotine base, etc.) into syringe, add to vial. Swirl gently.
6. **Draw full volume** back into syringe, fill pod.

### Temperatures
- **50-60°C**: Sweet spot. Dissolves quickly, no compound degradation.
- **>80°C**: Risk of degrading thermolabile compounds (etomidate, ester anesthetics).
- **Room temp**: Works for low concentrations (<30mg/ml) with patience.

## Dose Calculation

| Pod volume | Dose range | Effect |
|------------|-----------|--------|
| 1-1.2ml | 10-20mg/ml | Local numbing only (throat/lung anesthesia) |
| 1-1.2ml | 30-40mg/ml | Noticeable systemic effects (lightheadedness, floaty) |
| 1-1.2ml | 50mg/ml+ | Elevated toxicity risk — CNS excitation, seizure threshold lowered |

For a **noticeable systemic hit** from an ester anesthetic (amethocaine/procaine): ~30-40mg in 1ml pod.
For **numbing only with zero systemic risk**: 15-20mg in 1ml pod.

## Safety Thresholds

**Always compound with the specific compound's known max dose in mind.**
- Amethocaine (tetracaine) max safe inhaled dose: ~50mg absolute per session
- Premix larger batches for even dosing across multiple pods
- If the powder won't fully dissolve at your target concentration, reduce the dose — never use a suspension

## Compound-Specific Notes

### Amethocaine HCl (Tetracaine)
- Ester-type local anesthetic, ~8x more potent than procaine
- pH 4-5 in solution — acidic, generally compatible with acidic base mixes
- Systemic effects at 30mg+: dissociation, tinnitus, muscle twitching at high end
- Solubility in PG: ~100mg/ml at room temp, ~150mg/ml with heat

### Etomidate
- Free base (NOT a salt) — commercially formulated as free base in 35% propylene glycol (Amidate/Hypnomidate IV injection is literally etomidate in PG)
- PG is its native vehicle: 100+ mg/ml soluble at room temp. VG is a poor solvent — keep VG under 40-50% or it precipitates
- Ethyl ester — hydrolyzes above 80°C and in alkaline conditions. Shelf-stable in PG
- Metabolized by plasma esterases — short duration (~5-10 min)
- Inhaled dosing: 10-20 mg/ml pod = sedative range; IV induction equivalent is 0.2-0.3 mg/kg (15-25 mg adult) — treat inhaled as near-IV speed
- Watch: myoclonus at higher doses, adrenal cortisol suppression (11β-hydroxylase) even after single doses, respiratory depression when stacked with opioids/CNS depressants
