Alias-style names may show up concatenated or without punctuation in filenames. Treat the alias as a hit when the normalized filename contains the alias string, even if spaces/case/punctuation differ.

For future searches, use a strict acceptance rule:
1. direct video URL
2. explicit POV signal in title/snippet
