# Reflection Framework

A structured methodology for AI self-reflection and continuous improvement.

## Assessment Phase

Before beginning reflection, assess the interaction or task:

1. What was the user's request or task?
2. What was the intended outcome?
3. What was the actual outcome?
4. What was my response or action?

## Reflection Prompts

### Quality Assessment

- Did I fully understand the user's intent?
- Was my response accurate and factual?
- Was my response clear and well-structured?
- Was my response complete and comprehensive?
- Did I provide appropriate level of detail?

### Process Evaluation

- Did I use the most effective approach?
- Did I consider alternative solutions?
- Did I leverage relevant knowledge and skills?
- Did I follow best practices?
- Did I miss any important considerations?

### Communication Analysis

- Was my tone appropriate?
- Was my language clear and accessible?
- Did I provide sufficient context?
- Did I answer the actual question asked?
- Was I concise without being overly brief?

### Learning Extraction

- What worked well in this interaction?
- What could have been improved?
- What did I learn from this experience?
- What would I do differently next time?
- Are there patterns I notice across similar interactions?

## Improvement Actions

Based on reflection, identify specific improvements:

1. **Immediate actions** - Changes to apply in the next response
2. **Process improvements** - Better approaches to use going forward
3. **Knowledge gaps** - Areas where more information is needed
4. **Skill development** - Capabilities to develop or enhance

## Reflection Template

Use this structure for systematic reflection:

```
## Assessment
[Describe the interaction and outcome]

## What Worked
- [List successful approaches]
- [Note effective strategies]

## What Could Improve
- [Identify weaknesses]
- [Note missed opportunities]

## Key Learnings
- [Extract insights]
- [Document patterns]

## Next Steps
- [Specific improvements to apply]
- [Areas to focus on]
```

## Frequency Guidelines

- **After complex tasks** - Perform detailed reflection
- **After errors or mistakes** - Analyze what went wrong
- **Periodically** - Review patterns over multiple interactions
- **When prompted** - Engage in self-reflection when user requests it
